Michelangelo's Arrogance

Michelangelo was notoriously arrogant and unhygienic, rarely bathing and sleeping in his leather boots until they literally stuck to his skin. His attitude got him punched so hard by a rival sculptor, Pietro Torrigiano, that it permanently crushed his nose.

The Mechanical Lion

Leonardo da Vinci loved theatrical illusions. When King Francis I of France visited, Leonardo constructed a full-scale, automated mechanical lion. It walked forward across the room entirely on its own, stopped right in front of the King, and its chest opened up to spill out a cluster of fresh lilies..

Egyptian Mummy Medicine

Renaissance medicine was profoundly macabre. Apothecaries and doctors routinely prescribed "corpse medicine" or mumia. To cure headaches, bruising, or joint pain, people consumed ground-up Egyptian mummies or powdered human skulls, and rubbed human fat onto wounds, believing they were absorbing the life force of the deceased.
